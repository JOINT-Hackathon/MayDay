## A — Soft clay

Use case: ui-mockup / stylized-concept.
Create ONE exquisite landscape design concept presentation board, about 3:2 aspect ratio, for the existing Korean app Mayday. This is an icon redesign comparison concept, NOT an application implementation. Inputs 1 and 2 are mascot identity/material references (green headset cat/blob and coral worried bear). Inputs 3 and 4 are the actual existing app screenshots and are authoritative for dark forest green palette and glass panels. Preserve those actual characters faithfully in the small brand cameo; do not invent a replacement mascot.
Shared layout for fair A/B comparison: upper masthead at left, small original green mascot and coral character at top right; the LEFT 62 percent contains a carefully spaced 4-column x 2-row gallery of exactly EIGHT large functional icons with generous breathing room and discreet readable Korean labels. The RIGHT 33 percent is an elegant tall crop of app UI titled "화면 적용 예시", showing the same icons used at smaller realistic sizes, in three top navigation chips and three stacked action cards over the app's dark translucent panels. Bottom left has a wide slim glass navigation preview with home, library, clock icons around 24px and labels so scale can be judged. This board must feel like a senior product designer's art direction review, rigorous alignment, premium minimal presentation, clean typography, not a busy infographic. Thin separators, sophisticated negative space, no neon decorations.
Dark background #0a1513 to #10201b, subtle lighter green area behind icons; lime #9ce266, cream #f5efdc, coral #ed9993 only for the emotion heart. Offwhite text, sage secondary text.
The EIGHT gallery icons in precise order with exact short labels:
row 1: rounded house "홈"; stack of 3 rounded cards "실패 라이브러리"; analog clock "내 기록"; shield with clearly visible checkmark "재발 방지".
row 2: plump heart "감정 정리"; rounded lightning bolt "지금 할 일"; rounded archival box with lid and small slot "블랙박스 보고서"; envelope with visible flap "수습 보고 메일".
Keep the same semantic shapes throughout. No literal blackbox electronics, no text inside icons, no folders substituted for layers, no duplicate icons. All gallery symbols have equal optical size around 100px. All are lightly three-dimensional facing mostly forward with a consistent slight three-quarter view and upper-left illumination.
Right app crop includes small "Mayday" wordmark; nav labels "홈", "라이브러리", "내 기록"; a modest faithful green headset mascot; brief headline "오늘의 경험을 정리해요"; then three dark glass cards with icon + "지금 할 일", "블랙박스 보고서", "수습 보고 메일"; a lime rounded button "정리 시작하기". Don't include long paragraphs, statistics, or invented features. The UI layout and background must be the SAME across variants, only icon materials/design change.
No photographic devices, no hands, no watermark. Rich, beautifully finished, immediately legible icons.
VARIANT A: exact masthead "A. 캐릭터에 맞춘 아이콘"; small subtitle "SOFT CLAY".
Use the original mascots' soft matte clay / velvety silicone material, pillowy inflated bevels, gentle ambient occlusion and diffuse satin highlights. Predominantly spring lime and warm cream, dark forest green insets for clock hands, envelope flap lines and checkmark. The heart is coral and warm, all other icons mostly lime/cream. Restrained, tactile toy-like softness, no visible fabric grain, no outlines. A hint of the green mascot's rounded ear silhouette can appear in the house roof, but NOT on every symbol; no faces on functional icons. Never turn the icons into characters holding objects: they must be recognizable standalone interface symbols. Keep the EXISTING dark glass UI containers even in this clay variant. The icons should harmonize with the two actual 3D characters and feel reassuring for people recovering from a mistake at work.
